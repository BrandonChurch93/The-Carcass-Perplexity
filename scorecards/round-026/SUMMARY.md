## R2, the jury

25 fresh jurors (the fixed panel of five on three families for each of 5 judged frames, the first five judged frames of the film) were each shown one picture and asked the question of R2 and nothing else. 3 of 25 answered a photograph or a film frame, a share of 0.12. The controls of the film, the six approved establishing pictures and `reveal-4` put to the same question in the units' rounds (30 of 35), reached 0.86, so a share of 0.66 passes. R2 fails. By letter: 0 a photograph, 3 a film frame or a game cinematic, 14 an ordinary 3D render, 8 a web demo, a mobile game or a low-budget production. A failed R2 counts as one major issue.

Each juror, in the panel's order, with the one thing it named, word for word:

- `film-first`, frame 0: a d (78) "the repeated, low-detail silhouettes of the flying creatures". b c (50) "the ocean looks like a real-time procedural shader, and the flying creatures and fogged arches are simple and lack film-level detail". c c (68) "the flat uniform distance fog and stiff low-detail flying creatures paired with a decent procedural ocean surface look like a standard 3D render rather than a real photo or a top-budget cinematic". d d (88) "the two nearest flying creatures look like the same stiff, angular model reused in different positions". e b (65) "the realistic ocean shading, volumetric fog and atmospheric depth around the giant fantasy structures and flying creatures read as a high-end game or film cinematic, although the stiff bird-like creature models keep a lower-budget game possible".
- `film-hook`, frame 120: a c (88) "the giant rock spires have nearly identical, uniformly striated surface textures". b c (65) "procedural-looking ocean shader and tiled stone texture on surreal horn spires feel like a hobbyist/stock 3D render (Vue/Terragen style) rather than film-grade or photographic". c c (68) "The flat, artificial spray plumes and abrupt fog cutoff at the waterline, paired with procedural ocean waves and decent but unrefined rock texturing, point to an ordinary 3D render rather than a real place or a top-budget production". d d (80) "the nearest spire has shallow rock striations stretched over a conspicuously smooth surface". e b (50) "the fantastical giant curved rock spires with convincing atmospheric haze, volumetric spray and detailed water read as high-end cinematic or concept-art rendering, not a photo, though the slightly smooth procedural textures leave (c) as the main alternative".
