## Top issues

The critics and the jurors agree with each other, and the Builder agrees with them. One issue is declined in part (see below).

1. Severe, every pack of R1 and R3, R4, 9 jurors: the crust reads as a pattern. "regular comb-like grooves recur across the cooled surface and stay nearly black against otherwise smooth terrain, resembling a repeated stamped pattern"; "smooth, soft clay painted with dark reddish-brown procedural stripes instead of rough, granular dark volcanic rock"; "a smooth tan-brown surface with artificial swirling contour marks" where the picture has "dark, fractured basalt rock, loose rubble"; on the banks "sheared vertical clay with stretched vertical texture stripes"; "parts of the fissure read as a bright line on top of it rather than a recessed opening". Round 4's word was a smooth sheet; the relief is there now and it repeats.
2. Severe, R1 and R3, 5 jurors: the fall and the mouth, judged before the new stream. "an orange wireframe painted on flat dark plates"; "a flat ribbon with parallel stretched streaks and a hard rectangular break"; the mouth "a bright, hard-edged orange painted band around a flat black interior", "blocky, faceted arch walls and a straight horizontal lip". In motion (R4, severe twice): "the bright shapes in the fall change outline from one frame to the next with no coherent flow direction, so the fall boils and shimmers instead of moving downhill".
3. Severe, R1, heart-1, and R3: the melt. "flat procedural yellow-orange pools with crisp concentric red-and-grey stripes"; the spray "breaks into many detached, bright specks above a nearly uniform orange strip". In motion: "the glowing cracks are almost frozen. Only the glow of the right-hand pool pulses".
4. Severe, R1 and R3, heart-1: the left edge. "a long, straight-edged change in opacity slices through the steam, rock silhouette and background glow"; "a hard-edged vertical grey-and-black clipped artifact with a floating dark rock chunk". Found after the round by drawing the parts alone: one stretched face of the bench's lattice, a chunk of it hanging in the air, and the vent's steam cut by the bound of its volume.
5. Severe and major, R1, 4 jurors: the lava lights nothing. "cracks look like flat orange lines with pale halos that light nothing around them"; a juror: "the flat unshaded ground plane and lack of cast shadows or lava bounce light on the creatures".
6. Severe, R1, heart-3 and heart-4: what stands behind and in front. "the dramatic dark volcanic cliff and craggy slope behind the creatures are missing, leaving an empty flat peach-brown fog gradient"; "washed out with flat peach-brown midtones and weak blacks"; at heart-4 "a flat, glassy surface with blurry orange reflections and a ruler-straight horizon edge" where the picture has a rough dark floor out of focus with a glowing fissure.
7. Severe, R4, and major, R1 and R3, 7 jurors in passing: the animals. "the near creature holds the same pose for the whole shot except its head; body, legs and weight never shift"; "feet taper to points with weak ground-contact shadows, and several animals repeat the same splayed stance"; "dangling shred artifacts under the foreground torso, flat triangular and spade-like feet"; tendrils "thick, oversized pale translucent roots" where the picture has "fine golden-white glowing filaments".
8. Severe and major, R1 and R3, heart-2, judged before version C: the one that sank "bends in a smooth rubbery arch covered in thick tan patches, sitting on smooth grey extruded clay-like tubes"; "many lobes share uniform parallel ridges". In motion: "over almost 4 seconds nearly nothing changes".
