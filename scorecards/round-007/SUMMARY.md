## Top issues

1. Severe six times and major nine times, R1 and R3, 15 jurors: the lake's crust and its melt. At the line "the ropy, swirled pahoehoe crust has become regular parallel ridges that read as wind-rippled sand or a procedural stripe texture"; "smooth, clay-like procedural zebra stripes with uniform parallel ridges and no crisp basalt fractures, grit, or glassy surface variation"; "the lava fluid is a flat, smooth orange-to-yellow gradient with razor-sharp edges, no cooling crust plates or surface viscosity, and hard-clipped highlights with no bloom"; "the lava is thinner and dimmer, with no bright yellow core and no swirling skin, and it lights very little of the crust beside it". At the wide view "a fine repeating ripple pattern like sand instead of cooled pahoehoe slabs and plates" and "many short, evenly bright dashes and spot fires with hard edges that light nothing around them". At the feeding seam "pale, dusty, sandy-brown ground with sparse isolated short orange slivers and scattered pebbles, reading as desert rather than a live lava field". At the fall "uniform orange-red contour outlines painted over a smooth, clay-like dark grey chute". A juror: "the foreground lava crust is made of sharply bounded patches with the same evenly spaced grooves".
2. Severe twice and major three times, R1 and R3, 2 jurors: the mouth, its fall and the wall. "a single thin channel down a dark ridge and a smaller delta at the lower right, so much less lava and glow" where the picture has "a broad, branching bright cascade" that "pours from the cave lip over several steps and fans into a wide glowing delta"; the wall "a stack of horizontal sedimentary layers like a canyon wall, and the slope is lumpy with visibly faceted, low-detail geometry"; the arch "a flat orange-pink band, pasted-looking, with a dark lip and no light spill onto the wall beside it"; "a hard horizontal seam and flat grey shelf lip mark where the cave mesh meets the cliff wall"; the cave's inside "a flat featureless dark void".
