1. `R1-1`, set-A-03-b.png, the red brine surface and its foreground shoreline edge: "the water is a flat, matte pinkish-red plane with repetitive noise and ends in a hard, straight salmon-pink strip against the dark bank, losing the deep viscous rust brine, sky reflections, and crystalline white salt rim". Asked: "deepen the brine to glossy dark rust-red with organic surface undulations, add a rough crystalline salt crust along the foreground bank, and blend the wet shoreline with foam and small bubbles".
2. `R1-1`, set-A-03-b.png, the large right-side bubble and the left-side bursting splash: "the bubble looks like an opaque, smooth plastic dome with a hard flared rim, and the splash is a flat-shaded, clay-like opaque crown instead of translucent liquid". Asked: "make the bubble thin, glassy, and translucent with subtle surface grit and a wet meniscus, and render the splash as thin, translucent, jagged liquid sheets with foam and crisp specular highlights".
3. `R1-1`, set-A-01-b.png, the foreground brine channel, left bubbling pool, and meandering stream: "the foreground water looks matte and mud-like rather than reflective liquid, the left pool is pale milky pink with tiny dots instead of prominent dome bubbles and concentric ripples, and the channel runs straight to the horizon like a canal". Asked: "give the brine dark rust-red colour and glossy sky reflections, restore larger bubbles and strong concentric ripples in the left pool, and let the distant stream meander naturally across the salt flats".
