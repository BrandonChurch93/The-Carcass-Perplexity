What the Builder takes from it, looking at the frames beside the pictures. The critics and the jury say one thing first and they say it together: the rock. The approved pictures show a shaft of pale, weathered stone that curves like ribs, lit from within by warm steam. The frames show dark slabs with bevelled edges and textures pulled long across them, falling into black. 18 of the 25 jurors name that first. The second thing is the vapour, which stands over the pit as one soft shape and does not rise, pour or thin. The third is the animals, which read as copies of one rigid model. The fourth is the light: flat and beige where the pictures are low, gold and backlit. None of this is a surprise in a version 2, and the measures are green, so the place holds together and moves. What is missing is everything that makes a frame read as filmed.

## Top issues

1. Severe five times and major six times, R1 and R3, 18 jurors: the pit, its walls and its depth. "the eroded pale travertine cavity with curved rib-like rock arches and sunlit steam is replaced by a pitch-black void lined with dark blocky geometric slabs and an extra flat-topped volcanic vent on the far rim"; "the massive pale weathered curved rib-like rock ledges and billowing grey-white steam filling the pit are replaced by a pitch-black angular chasm with dark flat stepped blocks"; "the pale, weathered, rust-and-moss-stained calcified rib arches and fractured stone slab are replaced by dark charcoal-brown blocky low-poly terraces falling into near-black darkness"; "the rock reads as molded slabs, with angular cutouts, smooth bevels and thin bright cracks drawn over broadly uniform faces"; "similarly rounded slab edges and near-black gaps turn the pit into stacked set pieces rather than a continuous rock formation"; "the shelves disappear into near-black gaps with almost no reflected light, making separate layers read as cutouts"; "the texture smears over the rounded edges in broad stripes and the thin crack highlights are visibly stair-stepped"; "thin, jagged bright-white polygon and UV-seam lines run along the dark smooth rock ledges and corners". Jurors: "the crater wall looks assembled from flat polygonal ledges suspended in a black void"; "the cliff walls look like stacks of coarse polygon slabs with textures visibly stretched over their edges".
2. Severe five times, major four times and minor once, R4, R3 and R1: the vapour. "the cloud keeps one soft, blobby outline for six seconds and slides with the camera like a flat card. It does not rise, curl or thin, so the pit reads as a still set with a sprite laid on top"; "after four seconds of almost no change, the oval cloud suddenly grows into a big pale disc with a hard, soft-edged sprite outline, and a bright translucent band appears under it at 4.91 s. It pops instead of welling up"; "the pit vapor reads as a broad blur laid over the rocks and the distant band repeats the same curled profile"; "a broad, smooth patch of mist lies across the rim with a nearly horizontal lower edge and little visible breakup around the ledges"; "a large, opaque, blurry bright-beige fog patch hangs across the top of the frame, blocking the far rim and horizon, while the lower-left pit lacks the rising wispy steam"; the far plumes "hold nearly the same silhouette. They read as fixed shapes, not rising, wind-sheared columns"; the dark fume band along the rim "reads as a texture stuck to the rock, not as vapour".
3. Severe five times and major six times, R1, R3 and R4, 5 jurors: the waders, living and dead. "the gaunt, anatomically detailed pale fleshy bodies with visible ribcages, muscular joints, and fine wet pink tendrils draped in algae are replaced by smooth bulbous brown-mottled oval torsos on rigid pipe-like legs"; "the repeated dome torsos and hard angular knees look like copies of one rigid model; the closest legs end abruptly in the surface"; "the cylindrical legs appear to slot into the body beneath an abrupt overhanging shell"; "all three are locked in the same head-down feeding pose at once and barely change"; the dead one "a stiff inflated lumpy sack on top of flat ground with straight rigid stick" legs, and "it stays perfectly rigid while the camera pushes in. If it is alive it reads as a placed model. If it is dead, nothing around it (scavengers, flies, wet sheen, wind on its fronds) tells us so"; "the skin detail stretches into parallel ridges along the bend and bunches abruptly at the join"; the herd on the far flats "hardly change position or pose across 6 s".
4. Severe five times, R1: the light and the colour. "the warm golden low-angle sunlight and luminous backlit mist are replaced by flat pale beige light and isolated, opaque, blurry smoke puffs"; "the glowing golden sun, warm rim lighting on the creatures, drifting mid-ground steam, and snow-dusted cave cliff are replaced by flat beige light, no ground mist, and a smooth brown ridge with a stiff white fog band"; "the warm golden-hour backlighting, shimmering water reflections, deep chasm contrast, and soft wispy steam are replaced by flat desaturated beige fog with straight diagonal light streaks"; "flat clinical lighting with harsh black shadows on the left rock and zero depth of field".
5. Severe four times and major three times, R1 and R3: the ground, the shore and the flats. "the pale, craggy, multi-layered travertine shelves and pebble-strewn irregular banks are replaced by smooth dark-brown terrain with flat painted crack lines and an artificial smooth rounded mudbank"; "a visibly pixelated, low-resolution repeating polygonal mud-crack texture with glossy brown highlights"; "the out-of-focus wet foreground rock shelf framing the bottom is completely absent"; "the ground texture is dragged into long horizontal streaks and broad smears"; "the water-to-land transition turns into a smooth graphic stripe without a credible wet margin"; the far flats collapse "into long, parallel bands behind a straight transition".
6. Severe three times, major once and minor twice, R1, R3 and R4: the water. "the shallow winding turquoise-tinted flow over rocky shelves is replaced by a straight canal-like channel of opaque grey-blue water with harsh repetitive wave-bump glare streaks"; "a flat grey-green noisy plane with faint reddish smudges, harsh silver glare on the left"; "the bright airborne splash droplets and wet glistening leg ripples in the target are completely missing over flat uniform CG ripples"; "the regularly sized ripple texture continues through most of the surface without enough variation in direction or scale"; "the fine glints jump around from frame to frame like noise instead of moving with the ripples".
7. Severe twice, major five times and minor once, R1, R3 and R4: the red growth. "the wet, glossy, backlit crimson fronds with hanging water droplets are replaced by dry, opaque palm-like fronds"; "a sharp, dry red palm-like shrub on a smooth, stretched-texture pale ledge with sharp polygonal edges"; "the dense, low, dark-crimson tangled thickets nestled in the mist and rocks are replaced by sparse, bright-red spiky fronds sticking straight up"; "heavy speckled, dithered alpha-clipping noise around every leaflet"; "scattered short white vertical rain streaks float in the air instead of clear water droplets hanging from and dripping off the frond tips"; "the fronds are almost static between frames, with no sign of wind even though the vapour is moving".
8. Severe four times, R1: what stands in the frame and what does not. "the craggy snow-patched limestone cliff with its massive dark cave overhang is replaced by a smooth featureless brown ridge with no cave or snow"; in the view of the gill plumes "the tall, pale, long-necked stilt creature standing in the sunlit mist is completely absent"; down the shaft "instead of several small dark-silhouetted creatures far out on the sunlit reflecting flats, a single large, stiff, unnaturally smooth pale grey creature stands right beside the rim in sharp focus"; in the same view "the close-up wet pebbled rim, fleshy plant root, and weathered bone are missing, replaced by a pulled-back view".
9. Major twice, R4: the camera. "the move ends with the frame half-filled by dark, out-of-focus foliage over a flat wash of fog. It is a weak picture to end on, and the camera move has no clear target"; "the camera passes very close through the foliage, then drifts across the steps and ends on an empty ledge with the creature small and soft in the distance. The path feels floaty and the move ends without a subject".
