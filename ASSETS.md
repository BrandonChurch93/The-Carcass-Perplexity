# Assets

Every file in this repo that the build did not make itself, and the terms of every tool whose output appears in the film. `kit/06-ASSETS.md` has the rules.

## Approved pictures

Made by the reference runs on 2026-11-24 with an image model, with real public-domain photographs passed in as the standard for how a camera records light. Approved by Brandon the same day. The keys are listed in `keys` in `config/showcase.json`. The prompt, the inputs and the model of every picture are in `references/drafts/NOTES.md`, and the photographs are credited in `references/drafts/working/real/CREDITS.md`.
