# Assets

Every file in this repo that the build did not make itself, and the terms of every tool whose output appears in the film. `kit/06-ASSETS.md` has the rules.

## Approved pictures

Made by the reference runs on 2026-11-24 with an image model, with real public-domain photographs passed in as the standard for how a camera records light. Approved by Brandon the same day. The keys are listed in `keys` in `config/showcase.json`. The prompt, the inputs and the model of every picture are in `references/drafts/NOTES.md`, and the photographs are credited in `references/drafts/working/real/CREDITS.md`.

- Speech and sound effects published inside a film: allowed. STATED, Terms of Use s.1(c): a paid user "may use the Services for commercial purposes". STATED, Exhibit 4 s.2: output may be used "by incorporating it into films, trailers, advertising, video, games, applications, and other productions".

STATED, Exhibit 4 s.2: "you may not sell, resell, license, sublicense, distribute, or otherwise commercially exploit Output, or any part of it, on a standalone basis, or make Output available as isolated audio files or samples". - A voice made with voice design, used commercially: allowed under the licence of a paid plan. STATED, help centre: "All paid plans include a commercial license, provided you're not using Beta Services." UNKNOWN: whether the help centre's line "Voice Design is still experimental" makes it a Beta Service. The Beta Services Addendum did not load, and nothing that was read calls voice design beta.
