Image 1 is a layout map I drew in flat colours: the plan of one landmass seen from orbit. It is only a map. Keep the shape of its coast and the position of every feature. Do not copy its drawing style.
Images 2 and 3 are real photographs taken from spacecraft. The picture you make must be indistinguishable from them in kind: the same real ocean, real shallow banks, real cloud and haze, the same fine grain of terrain seen from very high. Do not copy what they show.

A real astronaut photograph, hand-held through a spacecraft window in high orbit, of a whole desert continent on an alien ocean world, seen from almost directly above late in the day. Natural colour, wide 16:9 frame.

By an accident of geology that no one on board can stop staring at, the continent has the plan of a human body lying on its back: a head at the left, a neck, broad shoulders, two long arms that end in hands, a chest, a narrow waist, hips, and two long legs that end in feet at the right, in true human proportion. It is unmistakable. It is also plainly land, thousands of kilometres of it: arid, ochre and bone-pale, old and worn, like Arabia or Baja California.

Read the map this way and render it as real terrain:
- The limbs are long mountainous peninsulas. A worn snow-dusted range runs the length of each one, with knots of high peaks at the shoulder, elbow, wrist, hip, knee and ankle, and dry plains, alluvial fans, dune fields and salt pans on either side. The hands and feet end in five narrow rocky peninsulas each, crumbling into chains of islets and reefs.
- The head is a high pale massif, with one perfectly round crater lake in it: deep blue centre, amber shallows, pale cliffs. Below it a small bay.
- Under the head, at the throat, a pale tidal flat with a line of seven dark blue holes rimmed in crimson, each trailing white vapour.
- The chest is ten pairs of long curved pale ridges with dark valleys between, either side of a narrow grey strait.
- Beside the strait, a black lava field with glowing orange fissures and a brown fume plume drifting toward the top of the frame.
- The waist is a shallow turquoise inland sea inside the coast, crossed down the middle by one knife-edged snow-white ridge of peaks. A dense storm cloud with violet lightning sits on the ridge.
- At the hips two curved ranges enclose a blood-red salt flat, red brine veined over white salt.

Every coast is irregular: capes, bays, deltas, spits, barrier islands, reefs. Pale turquoise shallows of uneven width fringe the land and fall away into deep dark blue ocean. Scattered cloud and cloud streets over the sea with their shadows, one big spiral storm far out at the lower right, thin cloud caught on the highest ranges, nothing hiding the land. Low sun from the right, long mountain shadows falling left, a soft band of sunglint on the water. The curved edge of the planet with its thin blue atmosphere and black space beyond cuts only the top left corner.

No people, no text, no labels, no borders, no spacecraft parts. Not a sculpture, not a skeleton model, not an illustration.
