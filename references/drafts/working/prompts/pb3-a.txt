Images 2 and 3 are real satellite photographs of desert coasts. Use them only as the standard for how real land, shallows, deep water and cloud look at this height: flat, fine-grained, slightly hazy. Do not copy what they show.

The frame is about two thousand kilometres wide. At this scale no single hill, cliff or cloud puff can be picked out. Mountain belts are fine wrinkled bands a shade paler or darker than the plains, cut by hair-thin branching dry valleys; plains carry faint dune fields, pale pans and alluvial fans; snow is a thin dusting on the highest crests only. Relief is flat to the eye: nothing is embossed, carved, sculpted or shiny, and no ridge casts a shadow you can see. The sun is high and to the right.

What is in the frame, left to right:
- Open ocean, deep dark blue, with fields of very fine white cloud stipple and thin cloud streets, each puff tiny, each with a pin-prick shadow on the water.
- The head: a pale, high, bare massif shaped like a skull seen in profile, face toward the top of the frame, with its jaw fallen open on a small bay. Pale limestone uplands with thin snow on the crown, dry valleys draining to a ragged coast with reefs and narrow turquoise shallows.
- In the eye socket of the skull, one round lake about a hundred kilometres across: a deep, dark blue centre, a ring of amber shallows inside its shore, and a pale cliff rim that is highest on the left side, toward the forehead, where it throws a thin shadow on the water. A thread of a river leaves the lake at its upper right and runs to the bay.
- The throat: a pale grey tidal flat under the jaw, with a loose scatter of dark round sinkholes of different sizes, each a black shaft with a thin crimson stain around its mouth. Thin white vapour rises from several of them and joins into one long faint plume drifting toward the top of the frame.
- The chest: on both sides, the ribs show through the land as long curved parallel ridges of pale rock with darker sandy valleys between, uneven in width and spacing, some broken, some half buried in sand. Down the middle of the chest, between the two sides, runs a narrow grey sea strait with pale tidal flats along its edges, from the throat toward the inland sea at the right edge of the frame.
- Across the middle of the chest, a wide matte black lava field with a ragged edge, like a dark stain on the land. A fine net of hair-thin dull orange cracks glows in its upper part. No flames.
- At the right edge, the edge of a storm: bright white cloud tops over a shallow turquoise inland sea.
