Image 1 is a photograph taken from orbit, looking almost straight down at part of a desert continent that has the shape of a giant human body lying in an ocean: at the left the skull-shaped head with its two round basins, then the neck, and at the right the shoulders and the long curved ridges of the chest. Make the same photograph again: the same view from the same height. Nothing on the ground has moved.

Keep exactly as in Image 1: all of the land; every coastline; both round basins and the lake; the turquoise shallows along the coasts; the black lava field and everything on it; the thin brown haze that drifts from the neck toward the top of the frame; the colours and the light; the edges of the frame, so that the picture still joins the pictures around it.

Change only the weather over the open sea. In Image 1 the dark blue ocean is sprinkled all over with thousands of tiny white specks in wavy rows. They look like glitter on the ripples of a pond, and they make the ocean look small. Remove every one of them. In their place put the open-ocean cloud that a spacecraft really sees from this height, as in Image 2. Most of the sea is clear, smooth and deep blue. Here and there lie thin, broken white lines of cloud, slightly curved, tens of kilometres long, in loose groups that run roughly the same way, and a few small bright clumps and feathery patches. Each cloud lays a faint dark shadow on the water just to its left. Large stretches of water have no cloud at all. The cloud lines are unequal in length, spacing and brightness; they never form a regular pattern, a grid, dots or ripples. No glitter, no sparkle, no waves, no long streaks of haze: from this height the surface of the sea is perfectly smooth.

Image 3 is a real satellite photograph of a desert coast. Use it only as the standard for natural colour and haze. Do not copy what it shows.

A real photograph through the window of a spacecraft: natural colour, slightly muted by haze, sharp from edge to edge. No people, no buildings, no roads, no text, no labels, no borders. Not a sculpture, not a relief model, not an illustration.
