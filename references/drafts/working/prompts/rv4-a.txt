Make one photograph taken by a crew member through the window of a spacecraft in high orbit, looking almost straight down at an entire continent on an alien ocean world, late in the local day. Natural colour, wide 16:9 frame.

The continent is the carcass of a titan: a giant of human shape that fell on its back ages ago and has weathered into land. It is geology now, not a figure and not a sculpture: a barren bone-pale and ochre continent thousands of kilometres long in a dark ocean, with a head, a neck, two shoulders, two arms with hands, a rib cage, a backbone, hips and two legs with feet, exactly where the map puts them. A viewer must see a human body at the first glance and a real continent at the second.

How it has weathered: every coast is ragged and real, with capes, bays, river deltas, sand spits, barrier islands and reefs. The fingers and toes are long narrow peninsulas breaking up into chains of islets. The long bones of the arms and legs are eroded mountain ranges with snow on the highest crests, flanked by foothills, dry river fans, dune fields and dusty plains. The knees, elbows and shoulders are high knotted massifs. Pale turquoise shallows of uneven width follow the coast and drop away to deep dark blue water.

The marked places, each exactly where the map shows it:
- The skull: a great pale massif in profile at the left end, face toward the top of the frame, jaw fallen open on a small bay. In its eye socket lies one perfectly round lake, deep blue at the centre with an amber ring of shallows, ringed by pale cliffs.
- The throat: a pale tidal shelf under the jaw with a line of seven dark round shafts rimmed in crimson, each trailing a thread of white vapour.
- The chest: the ribs are ten pairs of long curved bone-pale mountain ridges with dark valleys between them, either side of a narrow grey strait that runs down the middle of the chest.
- Beside the strait, on its upper side, a black lava field with glowing orange cracks and a brown fume plume drifting toward the top of the frame.
- The belly has fallen in and the sea has flooded it: a shallow turquoise sea inside the body's outline, crossed down its middle by the backbone, a single snow-white knife ridge of vertebra peaks. A dense storm cloud sits on the middle of that ridge, lit from inside by violet lightning.
- The hips: two great curved ranges. In the bowl between them a salt flat stained blood red, red brine veined across white salt.

Weather and light: scattered fair-weather cloud and long cloud streets over the ocean with their shadows on the water, one large spiral storm far out at sea at the lower right, thin cloud snagged on the highest ranges. Nothing hides the body. Low sun from the right throws long mountain shadows to the left and lays a soft band of sunglint on the sea. The curved edge of the planet, with its thin blue line of atmosphere and black space beyond, cuts only the top left corner.

No people, no text, no labels, no borders, no spacecraft parts.
