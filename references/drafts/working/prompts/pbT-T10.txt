The frame is about two thousand kilometres wide. At this scale no single hill, cliff, wave or cloud puff can be picked out. Mountain belts are fine wrinkled bands a shade paler or darker than the plains, cut by hair-thin branching dry valleys; plains carry faint dune fields, pale pans and alluvial fans; snow is a thin dusting on the highest crests only.
