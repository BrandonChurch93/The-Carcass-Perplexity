Image 1 is a soft, low-resolution enlargement of one part of a photograph taken from very high orbit. It shows the head, neck, shoulders and upper chest of a continent that has the shape of a giant human body lying on its back in an ocean. Make the same view again as a sharp photograph taken from a lower orbit, about a thousand kilometres up, looking almost straight down. This is the same camera on its way up; nothing on the ground has moved.

Keep everything exactly where it is in Image 1 and exactly the shape it is: every coastline, bay, cape and island, the two round basins on the head, the round lake, the dark hollow beside them, the rows of pale blocks and the channel between them, the pale flat on the neck with its dark holes and crimson streaks, the black lava field, each curved ridge, the storm cloud at the right edge, the edges of the frame. Keep the colours and the light. Do not move, add or remove any landform. Add only what a sharper, lower photograph would show.

Two things in Image 1 are faults of the enlargement, not things on the ground. Put them right:
1. The vapour. The pale flat on the neck gives off a thin haze. In Image 1 it is broken into scraps. Make it one continuous, faint, half-transparent plume that starts over the dark sinkholes, crosses the narrow water above the neck and drifts on toward the top edge of the frame, widening and thinning as it goes, and leaves the frame at the top just left of the shoulder coast.
2. The sea around the head shows faint straight edges where two exposures were joined, and the cloud changes abruptly along them. Remove every straight edge. The sea is one continuous surface and its small clouds run across it without a break.

Images 2 and 3 are real satellite photographs. Use them only as the standard for how real land, shallows, deep water and cloud look at this height: flat, fine-grained, slightly hazy. Do not copy what they show.

The frame is about two thousand kilometres wide. At this scale no single hill, cliff or cloud puff can be picked out. Mountain belts are fine wrinkled bands a shade paler or darker than the plains, cut by hair-thin branching dry valleys; plains carry faint dune fields, pale pans and alluvial fans; snow is a thin dusting on the highest crests only. Relief is flat to the eye: nothing is embossed, carved, sculpted or shiny. The sun is to the right.

What is in the frame, left to right:
- Open ocean, deep dark blue, smooth, with fields of very fine white cumulus in streets and open cells, each puff a dot with a pin-prick shadow on the water, a few thin streaks of cirrus, and wide stretches of clear sea.
- The head: a pale, high, bare massif shaped like a human skull lying face up, seen from above, the crown to the left and the jaw to the right. Two round basins side by side are its eye sockets: the upper one dry, dark, half filled with dune sand; the lower one holds a round lake. Right of them a dark ragged hollow is the nose. Right of that, two uneven rows of worn pale blocks face each other across a narrow bent channel of grey-green water: the teeth and the mouth. A ragged coast with narrow turquoise shallows.
- The lake in the lower eye socket, about a hundred kilometres across: a deep, dark blue centre, a narrow uneven shelf of amber shallows inside its shore, a pale cliff rim.
- The throat: a pale grey flat on the neck, right of the mouth, with a loose scatter of dark round sinkholes of different sizes, their crimson stains dragged into streaks, and the thin haze rising from them.
- The chest: on both sides, the ribs show through the land as long curved parallel ridges of pale rock with darker sandy valleys between, uneven in width and spacing, some broken, some half buried in sand. Down the middle of the chest, between the two sides, runs a narrow grey sea strait with pale tidal flats along its edges, from the throat toward the inland sea at the right edge of the frame.
- Across the middle of the chest, a wide matte black lava field with a ragged edge, like a dark stain on the land. A fine net of hair-thin dull orange cracks glows in its upper part. No flames.
- At the right edge, the edge of a storm: bright white cloud tops over a shallow turquoise inland sea.

A real photograph through the window of a spacecraft: natural colour, slightly muted by haze, sharp from edge to edge. No people, no text, no labels, no borders. Not a sculpture, not a relief model, not an illustration.
