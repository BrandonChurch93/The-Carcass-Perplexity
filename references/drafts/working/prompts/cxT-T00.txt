Image 1 is a soft, low-resolution enlargement of one part of a photograph taken from very high orbit. It shows part of a continent on an alien ocean world. The continent is the carcass of a titan of human shape that died ages ago and weathered into land: its bare bones are pale mountain belts, what is left of its dried hide is dark cracked plateau, and a dark stained sea lies in and around it. Make the same view again as a sharp photograph taken from a lower orbit, looking straight down. This is the same camera at a lower height; nothing on the ground has moved.

Keep everything exactly where it is in Image 1 and exactly the shape it is: every coastline, bay, cape and island, every ridge, basin and pit, every patch, band and bank of cloud and haze, every change of colour in the sea, the edges of the frame. Keep the colours and the light. Do not move, add or remove any landform or any large cloud. Add only what a sharper, lower photograph would show.

The other images are real photographs taken from orbit. Use them only as the standard for how real land, shallows, deep water and cloud look at this height: flat, fine-grained, slightly hazy. Do not copy what they show.

The frame is about sixteen hundred kilometres wide. At this scale no single hill, cliff, wave or cloud puff can be picked out.
- Pale land is bare limestone mountain country: belts of fine wrinkled ridges cut by hair-thin branching valleys, a dusting of snow on the highest crests, grey scree and pale fans at their feet, salt and sand in the basins. Where Image 1 shows smooth, waxy, scalloped, terraced or honeycombed surfaces, that is only the softness of the enlargement: replace it with this fine real terrain, keeping the same outlines.
- Dark red-brown land is low plateau, the dried hide: charcoal brown, rust and dull oxblood, cut by narrow canyons into irregular polygons like cracked mud seen from space, with dark screes and thin dry washes, its edges breaking down into the sea in cliffs, stacks and reefs.
- Relief is flat to the eye. Nothing is embossed, carved, sculpted, polished or shiny. A ridge shows only as a hair of light on its sunward side and a hair of shadow on the other.
- Coasts are ragged and real, with coves, spits, lagoons, reefs and small islands, fringed with pale jade-grey shallows of uneven width that fall away into deep water that is nearly black, a cold slate blue. Stained water keeps its milky green, ochre and rust swirls, with fine filaments and eddies inside them.
- All cloud is seen from above and lies flat: very fine white stipple, thin streets, soft bands, fog in bays, each puff tiny with a pin-prick shadow. The sun is low, to the right.

A real photograph through the window of a spacecraft: natural colour, cold, slightly muted by haze, sharp from edge to edge. No people, no text, no labels, no borders. Not a sculpture, not a relief model, not an anatomy chart, not an illustration.
