Images 2 and 3 are real photographs taken from orbit. Use them only as the standard for how real mountains, desert, shallows and deep water look at this height: flat, fine-grained, slightly hazy. Do not copy what they show.

The frame is about a thousand kilometres wide; the lake is about a hundred kilometres across. At this scale single hills and cliffs are still too small to pick out. Mountain ranges are finely wrinkled bands; valleys are hair-thin branching threads; snow is a thin dusting on the highest crests; plains carry faint dune fields, pale pans and alluvial fans. Relief is flat to the eye: nothing is embossed, carved, sculpted or shiny. The sun is to the right and getting low, so the light is a little warm and only the highest ridges throw short soft shadows toward the left.

What is in the frame:
- The skull massif fills the middle of the frame: a high, bare, pale limestone plateau, the dome of the skull at the left and bottom, the face toward the top right. Thin snow on the crown. Dry valleys drain outward to a ragged coast with reefs, small islands and narrow turquoise shallows.
- The eye: one round lake left of centre and above centre. A deep, dark blue centre; a ring of amber and gold shallows inside its shore, finely streaked toward the middle; a pale cliff rim all round, highest on the upper left, toward the forehead, where it throws a thin dark shadow on the water. At its upper right the rim is broken by a narrow gap, and a thread of a river runs from there to the bay.
- The face of the skull, weathered but readable. Right of the lake, a broad peninsula reaches toward the top right: the nose and upper jaw. Between the lake and the tip of that peninsula lies a dark, dry, pear-shaped basin of bare rock, like the hollow of a nose. Below the lake a narrow pale ridge curves toward the right like a cheekbone.
- Below the peninsula a narrow curved bay lies like an open mouth. Along both shores of the bay stand rows of short blunt pale headlands and sea stacks, side by side like worn teeth: uneven, some broken, some missing, each one a real cape with surf-white shallows at its foot.
- Beyond the bay, the lower jaw: a long, lower, darker ridge that hinges to the skull near the bottom right and runs up toward the throat.
- Across the dome of the skull, one or two very thin zigzag dry canyons, like the seams of a skull, half lost in the wrinkled plateau.
- At the top right, under the jaw, the corner of a pale grey tidal flat with a few dark round sinkholes, each a black shaft with a thin crimson stain around its mouth, and a little thin white vapour.
- Open ocean, deep dark blue, at the left and along the top, with fields of very fine white cloud stipple and thin cloud streets.
