2. Judging by the planetary curvature and weather systems, the skeleton spans roughly 2,500 to 4,000 km (1,500 to 2,500 miles) from skull to foot, with the camera in space several hundred to a couple thousand kilometers above the surface.
3. Aside from the geologically impossible anatomy (intact ribs, teeth, and five digits on every hand and foot), several details look fake or small-scale for orbit: the glowing orange lava spot in the ribs would have to be miles wide to shine like that in daylight, the lumpy white cloud plume on the spine has oversized cumulus puffs like local smoke seen from a plane, the thick red channels in the pelvis look like veins or poured dye on a miniature, and cloud wisps conveniently stream off the skull like hair.
