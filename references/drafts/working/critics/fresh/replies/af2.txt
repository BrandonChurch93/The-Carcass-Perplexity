Critic report on IMG_3391 to IMG_3394. I checked each pair by cropping the wider image, scaling it up and laying it over the closer one at 50%, then making a blurred difference map. All images are 1672x941.

1. NESTING (x and y are fractions of the wider image)
- 3391 inside 3392: x 32–59%, y 46–73%. That is a box about 27% wide, centred on the water-filled crater, so the step is about 3.7x. The overlay is almost exact. The lake outline, the yellow-ochre shore ring, the small notch at the top right of the ring, the white mist blowing over the lake's lower left, the cracked white salt patch at the upper right and the corner of teal sea at the bottom right are all in the same places. The mean colour of the matching region is identical (RGB 140/121/103 in both). That means 3392 contains a shrunken copy of 3391 and was painted outward from it. Small faults: in the difference map the cliff rim and the inner edge of the ring are a few pixels off, so the ring's thickness and the lake's edge wobble slightly. In 3391 the cliff is sharp only on the upper left; at 3392's scale the rim reads as an even ring all the way round. The terrain just outside the box in 3392 has a crisper, more "melted-wax" texture than the soft, hazy terrain inside it. That is a faint change in sharpness and texture at the border of the box, and I found no hard edge.
- 3392 inside 3393: x 9–60%, y 34–84%. The box is about 51% wide, so the step is about 2x. The overlay is near-exact. Both craters (the dry one with a white salt pan, the wet one), the dark rough hollow between them, the narrow inlet channel, the reddish plain with dark pits and red streaks, the coastline, the cloud bands at left and bottom, and the haze at the top right all match. The colour means are again identical (86/93/97). Differences: the small dark pits on the red plain lose definition, and a few of the smaller pits are barely there in 3393. The coastline and cloud edges are slightly out of register. The most serious fault is a straight north–south line at x ≈ 60%, exactly the right edge of the box. There, the red neck plain stops abruptly against the ridged terrain and lava to the right, with a pale vertical ridge and a small orange-red dot sitting right on the line. That is a visible seam.
- 3393 inside 3394: x 2.5–42%, y 34.5–74%. The box is about 40% wide, so the step is about 2.5x. The overlay is again near-exact, with identical colour means (77/89/97). The dust plume, the black lava fields with glowing orange cracks, the green river channel between them, the white cloud streak over the teal water and the snow-streaked northern ridge all line up. Differences: the ridged "rib" pattern has bright stripes in the difference map, so the rib count and spacing were partly redrawn. The orange glowing points become faint specks. I found no hard seam on the top, bottom or right edges of the box. The narrow channel at 3393's right edge opens into a broad teal lagoon full of clouds just outside the box. That is consistent with 3393, but it is new information.

2. CONTINUITY
Geometrically, all three steps nest, because each wider frame is built around a scaled copy of the closer one. The colours match exactly, so no step looks like a cut to a different place.
- The weakest geometric point is 3392 to 3393, because of the straight vertical seam at the neck/body boundary. In a slow zoom the eye will catch a straight line on otherwise organic terrain.
- 3391 to 3392 has a mild change in sharpness and texture at the border of the box.
- 3393 to 3394 is the biggest change in what the viewer understands. The land suddenly turns out to be a human body, and the rib ridges shimmer because they were redrawn. It holds as one place, but it feels like a reveal rather than a plain continuation.

3. REAL LARGE PLACE OR MINIATURE?
- 3391 is the most convincing as a real crater lake seen from the air. The cliff rim, gullies, salt crust and the mist over the water give it scale. Against it: the terrain has an even "crumpled cloth/clay" wrinkle everywhere with no roads, vegetation or hierarchy of erosion, and the warm, low-contrast haze is a bit tilt-shift-like.
- 3392 reads as an orbital view, because the small puffy cloud bands and the shallow teal shelf give scale. It starts to look like a sculpted object: two round craters as eyes, a dark "nose" hollow and red blood-like streaks with spatter dots. The ridge texture is uniform and looks moulded.
- 3393 is borderline. The clouds and the dust plume say "big", but the rib ridges are too regular, parallel and evenly spaced to be geology, and they look carved. Lava glowing with bright orange lines in full daylight from that height is implausible and looks like a lit model.
- 3394 shows a planet: the Earth's limb and black space at the top left, a hurricane spiral at the bottom right and sun glint at the upper right. The hurricane is roughly the size of the torso, which makes the landmass thousands of km across. The continent itself reads as a deliberately posed giant body, more land art or sculpture than geology. It has hands with separate fingers and perfectly paired limbs.

4. 3394: SHAPE AND MAIN PLACES
The land is shaped like a human figure or corpse lying spread-eagled, with the head at the far left (about x 8–20%, y 49–65%). Both arms go out from the shoulders, one up-right to a hand with fingers (about 52%, 10%) and one down-right to a hand (about 55%, 87%). Two legs run to the right, ending in feet at about 82%, 16% and about 85%, 72%. Teal shallows run along all the coasts.
- Skull island. The dry crater with a white salt pan is one eye socket; the water-filled lake with an ochre ring (the place in 3391) is the other. The dark rough hollow between them is the nose, and the narrow inlet channel runs beside them.
- Neck. A pale plain with red, vein-like streaks and dark pits, with the straight seam at its eastern edge.
- Ribcage. Ridged, snow-streaked rib terrain on both sides, with two black lava fields with glowing orange fissures and smoke at the top of the ribs. A green-teal river runs between them into a square teal lagoon with clouds, which forms the chest cavity.
- Belly. A red-veined, white and pink mottled patch at the centre of the torso, about 47–52%, 42–60%. It looks like an organ or a wound.
- Other features. A long dust plume blows in from the top left onto the neck, and there is the hurricane, the planet's edge and the sun glint.
