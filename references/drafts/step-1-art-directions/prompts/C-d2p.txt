Two reference images are supplied, with different jobs.
Image 1 is a wide photograph of a real place. The new picture is taken in that same place, at the same moment, in the same light and weather. Keep its landmarks, materials, colours and atmosphere exactly consistent.
Image 2 is an unrelated real photograph. Use it only as the standard for how a real camera records light, haze and exposure. Do not copy its subject or anything in it.

Make a new 16:9 photograph: a closer shot from the same big-budget natural-history film about an alien world, taken with a 135 mm lens from a low camera on the ash slope in Image 1, about fifteen metres from the animal. It must be indistinguishable from real location photography: one plane of focus, the subject sharp, everything nearer and farther soft.

IN FOCUS. The heavy eyeless animal from Image 1, standing at the glowing sac, seen from three-quarters behind. Its head is lowered toward the glow, so the side of its face and one shoulder are lit cyan-green and its back is a dark shape against the dusk. It has thick wrinkled hide with broad dark and pale bands, smooth skin where eyes would be, and mud dried on its legs. Around its feet, thin pale threads of tissue have risen out of the ash; several lie across its feet and one is drawn tight around a hind leg. It has not noticed. A few tiny pale flyers circle in the light. The frame cuts off the far side of the sac.

BEHIND, SOFT BUT RECOGNISABLE AS IMAGE 1. The narrow stream and its reflections; the dark valley with one dim far glow; the plain cliff; the dusk sky with its narrow band of dull orange.

WHAT MAKES IT REAL. The same single light source and weather as Image 1. Real materials with weight and wetness, small flaws, dirt and asymmetry; nothing carved, patterned, glossy or symmetrical; nothing repeated. The subject is caught as found, not arranged for the camera, and part of it is cut off by the edge of the frame. Natural exposure with gentle highlights and open shadows, no added sharpening, no glow added in post, restrained colour, fine film grain.

No people, no buildings, no equipment, no text, no watermark, no border.
