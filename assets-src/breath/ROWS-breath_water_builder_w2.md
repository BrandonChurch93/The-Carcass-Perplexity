# Assets of breath_water_builder_w2

No file is added and none is removed. Everything the water draws is computed in the build, in `src/places/breath/water/`:
the wave bands (`maps.ts`), the stream over the bed (`flow.ts`), the sheet (`shaders.ts`) and the skin along the waterline (`edge.ts`).
The rings and the contact round legs and mops are read from the waders folder (`src/places/breath/waders/ripples.ts`).
