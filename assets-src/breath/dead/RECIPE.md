# The dead wader's form

Both files are made by `python3 assets-src/breath/dead/make_lift.py` (numpy, scipy, Pillow; run from the root of the repository) from
the working picture of the dead wader, `public/assets/breath/mat/carcass.jpg` with its relief `carcass-h.png`. No model was called.

## public/assets/breath/dead/carcass-lift.png

927 x 424, the picture halved. Red: how high the skin stands off the rock, 0 to 0.5 m. Green: the cover. Blue: how open a place is to
the sky (the folds are dark). The limbs are the outline blown up; the trunk is a slope from the belly up to the arch of the ribs.

## public/assets/breath/dead/carcass-slope.png

927 x 424. Red and green: the slope of the skin across and down the picture, metres a metre, -3 to 3 (128 is level).
