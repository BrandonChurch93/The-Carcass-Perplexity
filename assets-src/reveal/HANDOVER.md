## 5. What a newcomer would trip over

- Cost follows the amount of shader code compiled into a program, not what a guard skips: a branch that never runs still costs. The land's three programs and the sky's cut both come from this. Any new term can put the unit over at all three cap frames.
- Single cost runs drift by 0.3 to 0.7 s and the machine slows through a wave. Numbers of another session decide nothing. `/root`'s test: one session, `origin/main` and the head back to back, best of three (`--runs 3`), and the cap is 5 percent over main in that session. Run it on the lead's machine before a merge, not after: the lead merged the sky's V3 on the builder's pairs and then found the unit 2 to 5 percent over main, inside the cap but by little, until the land's cut.
- Pushing works on some minutes and fails on others ("could not read Username"). Try at every commit; when it fails, send a bundle: `git bundle create /tmp/agent-p8-reveal-polish-v<N>.bundle origin/main..agent/p8-reveal-polish`.
- A step of the fallback chain has three tries and no fourth; then the next step starts. The old part stays in the film until a try beats it across the whole part. Nobody in the team marks a part; the lead reports the step to `/root`.
- `src/places/eye/handoff.json` and `src/places/reveal/handoff.json` must stay byte equal, and any change the Eye can see needs the Eye's lead's yes first. The Eye draws the lid through `lidOver` of `join/lid.ts`, so a change there changes the Eye's frames 9488 to 9540 even when no Eye file changes.
- `tests/rise-core.test.ts` line 161 turns red if the hold rises to 0.02 percent a frame; 0.014 is in. `tests/rise-join` "gone by the first key" passes by numbers only: `lidAt` and `erodeFrames` are kept for the tests.
- `land/relief-a.png` is read by `sky/index.ts` (line 511): the land tells the lead when it changes, the lead tells the sky. It did not change in this wave. `shared/anchors.ts` lines 52 to 91 are lent to the climb by name.
- Every texture read in `WEATHER_GLSL` counts twice, once in the land's cloud shadow and once in the sky, and a guard on a uniform saves nothing.
- The dark mottle in the sun's glow at 10027 is the land's sea glitter, not cloud shadow (the sky builder found it stays with the shadows off).
- `src/places/reveal/index.ts` belongs to nobody: builders ask the lead.
- From 9541 to about 9565 the deep water the two places share lies on the lid's centre, 16 km west of the rise's lake centre, and covers the rise's west shore until it fades. At hd the Eye draws the lid in its half-size pass, so 9540 is a little softer than 9541 there; at uhd both read 720 rows.
- `dist` follows the branch last built. Check out the unit's branch and build before any render for a report. One render at a time, never a build while one runs.
- Builders judge at 400 px or on a crop; the lead must look at both. Three sky versions read right at one size and wrong at the other.
- No em dashes and no exclamation points in any text; every commit ends with the one trailer.
