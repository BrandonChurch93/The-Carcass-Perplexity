# Recipes of the Eye's rock

The material pictures of the cliffs, the gravel and the stones of the Eye. None was made for the Eye: each is a copy, byte for byte, of a material picture of the Heart, which the sheet allows. The shaders of the Eye read them for grain and relief only and give the stone its own pale colour.

## `public/assets/eye/land/cliff.jpg` and `public/assets/eye/land/cliff-nh.png`

Made by: `cp`. Copies of `public/assets/heart/mat/cliff.jpg` and `public/assets/heart/mat/cliff-nh.png`.
From: the Heart's pictures; their own recipe is in `assets-src/heart/`.
Prompt or command: `cp public/assets/heart/mat/cliff.jpg public/assets/heart/mat/cliff-nh.png public/assets/eye/land/`
Takes: none made.
Why this one: bedded rock with ledges and rubble, read at two sizes for the faces of the socket and the wall of the shaft.
Date: 2026-11-25
