## `public/assets/eye/lake/matflat.jpg`

Made by: the image model of Perplexity Computer (`generate_image`, default model), then sharp 0.34 (`sharp(src).jpeg({ quality: 84 })`), no other change.
From: `assets-src/eye/fluid/matflat-take1.jpg`, the generated picture as it came (kept as a JPEG at quality 93). One picture was passed to the model with the prompt: `references/approved/6-eye/eye-0-establishing.jpg`, for colour and material only.
Prompt or command: asked at 1:1, came 1671x941: "Seamless tiling top-down orthographic aerial photograph, looking straight down from high above, of a shallow hot-spring mineral flat covered in microbial algal mats, like the apron around a geothermal pool. Irregular patches of matte amber, ochre, rust-orange and brown bacterial mat with fine mottled granular texture, separated by a branching network of narrow dark wet drainage channels of different widths that wander and fork, and scattered small islands of pale cream-grey mineral crust. Patches of many different sizes, no dominant direction, no parallel streaks, no stripes. Colours and material taken from the orange flats in the reference photo (image 1 is the reference for colour and material only). Flat even overcast light, no shadows, no horizon, no sky, no reflections, no water surface glare, no animals, no text. Fills the whole frame edge to edge, uniform scale across the image, photorealistic, sharp fine detail."
Takes: 2 made, take 1 kept. Take 2 is not committed.
Why this one: it has no lie. Its patches are of every size, its dark channels wander and fork, and it has small pale islands, so `src/places/eye/lake/bed.ts` can lay it by the ground under the mats seen from the rim (frames 8305 to 8519) and from over the centre (9385 to 9540) as mottle, channels and islands inside a patch. Take 2 had larger and fewer patches with a faint grain from left to right.
