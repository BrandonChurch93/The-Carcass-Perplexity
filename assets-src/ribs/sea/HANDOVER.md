Both shaded by `rigShade`, open sails pass the sky. Perched hides run from grey brown to cream by seed, one in three in shade; `Puppets` has a `shade` mesh (the same sheets 1.16 times larger, just behind, darkening the rock). Sitters rock, shift, preen and shake, each in its own time: 34 percent of `ribs-3` moves with the camera held (motion diagnostic, uhd draft, with the lead's 2752cff merged). The built animal (`wings/animal.ts`) is kept only for the two far landers and the runner.
- `drowned` (`drowned/index.ts`): a relief plate from `mat/carcass-hung.jpg`, turned to the ribs-05 lens, shaded by the place's light, stained, wet toward the bottom, a dark copy behind for contact, 18 weed strands across the legs, crabs.
