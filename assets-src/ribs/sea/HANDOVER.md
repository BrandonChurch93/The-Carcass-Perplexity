## How each part is drawn

- `sea`, `surf` (`sea/index.ts`, `sea/shaders.ts`, `sea/tide.ts`, `sea/spray.ts`): one displaced sheet of swell at `LEVELS.sea` with foam from two noise pictures made at creation (`tChop`, `tFoam`), shaded by `rigShade`. Surf is instanced cards (`Spray`) thrown where the swell meets bars and blade feet.
- `runnels` (POOL shaders in `sea/shaders.ts`): one sheet at `LEVELS.pool` over the lead's bed (`landAt().r + bedFine`); depth = pool minus bed; bars of bare sand cut out; mirror of sky by `skyCloud`, glare path to the sun, rings and a leg mirror from `uStands` (24 rings at most).
- `bore` (BORE shaders, `sea/index.ts`): one mesh riding `BORE_FRONT`, profile `pow(uc, 1.6)` with a thrown lip and a 48 m foam skirt; face of broken white with dark windows (`pane`); six groups of spray cards (BURST, CREST, FACE, PLUME, AHEAD, LIP: 520 small heads boiling on the crest that hide its drawn edge and read as the foam mass).
- `kite-wings` (`wings/animal.ts`, `wings/index.ts`): one instanced animal of tubes (body, neck, tuft, legs) and sail fans, posed in the vertex shader from per-instance data written each frame as a pure function of time. Skin from `mat/wing.jpg` and `mat/wing-nh.png` at three scales, tinted by its mean. 44 feeders, 78 colonists, 560 specks, fliers.
- `drowned` (`drowned/index.ts`): its own body of tubes and torn cloth hung on the lead's weed at `DROWNED`, 64 draped strands, drips, crabs, two old rib cages. Skin from `mat/wing` laid by the place on the face.

No picture was generated by me. The Heart's hide copies are deleted; `assets-src/ribs/wings/RECIPE.md` went with them.
