# Recipes of the Ribs: land

The material pictures of the land. Each began as one generated picture of a flat surface seen straight on under even light. The script makes each one tile and writes a colour picture and a data picture (slope x, slope y, height) from it.

Made by: the image model of Perplexity's `generate_image` (default model), then `assets-src/ribs/land/make-materials.mjs` on Node with the `sharp` of `package-lock.json`.
Command: `node assets-src/ribs/land/make-materials.mjs`. No picture was passed to the model with a prompt.
Date: 2026-11-25

## `public/assets/ribs/land/rock.jpg` and `rock-nh.jpg`

From: `assets-src/ribs/land/rock-take1.jpg`. Takes: 1 made, take 1 kept.

```
A seamless, tileable texture photograph: a straight-on, orthographic view of a weathered sea cliff face of pale ivory-grey limestone that fills the whole frame edge to edge. Irregular horizontal bedding layers of unlike thickness, small ledges, vertical cracks and joints, pitted and salt-worn faces, a few broken-out hollows. Patches of rust-orange lichen and thin white streaks of bird droppings running down from the ledges. About 30 metres of cliff across the frame, so the detail is fine. Flat, even overcast light, no cast shadows from outside the frame, no sky, no horizon, no sea, no plants, no people. Real location photography, natural restrained colour, no sharpening.
```

Why this one: bedded pale stone with lichen and white runs at the scale of the colony's ledges, evenly lit so the film's own light does the shading.
