# Recipes, the sky of the Spine (spine_sky_builder_w2)

The whole sky as the ridge's lenses see it is one picture, read by bearing and height in `src/places/spine/air/sky.ts`: 120 degrees round the horizon, from 4 degrees under the level to 44 over it. It is a source of sky, never the place: no rock, snow, spire, kite or bolt is in it, the strikes light it at their own frames, and what glows in it is held dark until a light of the rig reaches it. It is not held still: every place of it is carried along a flow field made from the picture itself, and torn scud on a level 1500 m up crosses before it and shifts against it as the lens moves.
