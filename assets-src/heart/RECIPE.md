# Recipes of the Heart

The material pictures of the Heart. Each began as one generated picture, 1254 px square, of a flat surface seen straight on under even light. The script makes each one tile and writes a colour picture and a data picture from it.

## `public/assets/heart/mat/materials.json`

Made by: `assets-src/heart/make-materials.mjs`.
From: the eight material pictures in `references/drafts/working/heart-materials/`.
Prompt or command: `node assets-src/heart/make-materials.mjs`
Takes: 1 run.
Why this one: it records the numbers of the script and the linear mean colour of each picture, which the surfaces use to keep their albedo in a real range.
Date: 2026-11-25

## What the script does

With the numbers it holds in `RECIPE` at its top:

1. Each picture is reduced to 1024 px square.
2. It is made to tile, one axis at a time: the middle stays, and within 14 percent of each edge it fades into a copy of itself shifted by half its size.
3. The colour picture is saved in sRGB as a JPEG at quality 92 with full colour resolution.
4. A height is taken from the light of the picture: the picture blurred by 1.2 px less the picture blurred by 5 percent of its size, scaled so one standard deviation is 0.2, centred on 0.5.
5. The data picture is linear, 8 bits: red and green hold the slope of that height across and down, 0.5 for level, and blue holds the height.
